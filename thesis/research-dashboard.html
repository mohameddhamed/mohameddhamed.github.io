Experimental Rig & Verification Methodology

To ensure scientific repeatability, each trial was executed with automated JVM warm-up phases (120 seconds of sustained load) prior to measurement. Telemetry was collected via JDK Flight Recorder (JFR) using customized event configurations:

  • jdk.VirtualThreadPinned: Threshold set to 0 ms to capture every carrier thread pinning event.
  • jdk.VirtualThreadSubmitFailed: Monitored to detect ForkJoinPool queue overflow and worker rejection.
  • jdk.CPULoad: Captured at 100ms intervals to evaluate OS scheduler context switching vs. user-mode fiber scheduling.
  • Load generation: Distributed Locust and JMeter runner nodes operating on an isolated 10 GbE local backplane.
